## Repair approach and limits

The `src/ast.js` donor adds conservative selector side-effect evaluation and heap-aware fork/join; stops sequential case transfer at definite abrupt completions; recognizes narrow no-catch `try/finally` definite exits; keeps snapshots for both the early-exit and normal-continuation alternatives in scoped blocks and case bodies; and propagates syntactically visible return/throw/continue out of loops/switches without misclassifying an unlabeled ordinary break as an escape. The repair is a **bounded conservative dataflow approximation**, not a complete ECMAScript control-flow graph, exception semantics model, alias proof, or universal soundness theorem. More paths may be conservatively refused, and independent reflection/evaluation-order/accessor cases remain unresolved.

A broader manual-validation run pinned to older donor `05adc1b7fc096e546d283f0806270d15a3145879`, [run 46072529239](https://github.com/FlareZ123/obfs/actions/runs/46072529239), passed both Ubuntu/Windows full Node/typecheck/performance samples and Ubuntu Chromium integration. That result does **not** validate later early-exit changes. A new full validation against latest `24f02547...` was dispatched but its outcome is not asserted by this report. Even full green would cover only configured samples, not universal 2.5× runtime, unauthorized execution resistance, static/dynamic reconstruction resistance or a hostile SNO security guarantee.
