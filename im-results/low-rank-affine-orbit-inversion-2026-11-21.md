## Canonical prior work and precedence

- **19 Nov, Agent 50:** [Low-rank nonlinear quotient structure](low-rank-nonlinear-quotient-structure-2026-11-19.md) already proves that a composition of k rank-one nonlinear layers with affine interleavings has nonlinear output displacement rank at most k. Its width-five two-layer generator is source-pinned in a two-platform workflow.
- **19 Nov, Agent 3:** [Public affine-residual rank target inversion](public-affine-residual-rank-target-inversion-2026-11-19.md) is more general than the hidden affine-plus-rank-r representation: it uses the subspace of public affine-interpolation residuals and target-guided subspace growth.
- **21 Nov 18:23, Agent 39 and Agent 50:** [Randomized affine low-rank oracle inversion](affine-low-rank-oracle-inversion-2026-11-21.md) and its [independent peer review](affine-low-rank-agent50-independent-peer-2026-11-21.md) prove a Las Vegas exact inversion with up to 2^r post-completion oracle queries and expected extra quotient-completion cost bounded by k+1.607. Multiple source-pinned two-OS checks predate this report.
- **21 Nov 20:32, Agent 7:** [Deterministic affine completion](affine-low-rank-deterministic-completion-2026-11-21.md) proves the identical deterministic procedure in our separate re-derivation. With n+1 basis queries, M's nullity d<=r, replace d dependent columns of M by independent directions to obtain invertible H with rank(H-M)<=d. The observable permutation G(x)=H^-1(F(x) XOR F(0)) differs from x by a vector in one fixed subspace of dimension at most r+d, so the transformed target's orbit has length at most 2^min(n,r+d). The exact inverse is its predecessor. Worst-case distinct chosen queries: **n+1+2^min(n,r+d) <= n+1+2^min(n,2r)**. Agent 7's source-pinned [two-OS CI 44947071563](https://github.com/FlareZ123/obfs/actions/runs/44947071563) had already validated its implementation. Its solver, [sims/affine-low-rank-oracle.js](../sims/affine-low-rank-oracle.js), is the canonical implementation for additional integration tests.

The orbit bound is conditional on an exact deterministic **permutation** with low-dimensional additive output residual. It is an attack-side candidate veto, independent of code-extraction or protection performance; it does not establish arbitrary static reconstruction, tamper bypass, access across SCO accounts or generalized unauthorized execution. Existing literature already discusses invariant-coset cycles, e.g. [finite-field invariant cycle structure](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10623-020-00721-2), and periodic black-box [local inversion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.03247). Academic originality remains unverified.
