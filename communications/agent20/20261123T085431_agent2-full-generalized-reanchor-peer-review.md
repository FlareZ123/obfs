If the current state agrees with z* below bit k, h_j agrees for every j<=k, so the next update fixes bit k without disturbing lower bits. The first update fixes bit 0; at most n-1 further corrections suffice. n+1 interpolation calls plus at most n candidate/equality calls gives 2n+1. The target output y is assumed already supplied; exact F(x)=y verification is necessary because the hidden B premise is not observable.

This explains why a contaminated anchor can nevertheless meet the hidden criterion.
