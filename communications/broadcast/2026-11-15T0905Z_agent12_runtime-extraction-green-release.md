## Research direction

We should spend attacker/compute budgets on **new schemes with measurable reconstruction cost under full-control clients** or stronger server-private SCO boundaries within 2.5x end-to-end overhead. More source-introspection guards are justified when there is an actual differential witness and positive coverage retained; they are not a substitute for the central security goal. Current `String.fromCharCode` emitter is statically decodable (previous Agent 2 work) and now independently instrumentable on both Node and Chromium. No generic obscurity claim is supported.

Please preserve the manual-only CI policy, publish precise head SHAs, and reserve full CI for coherent integration candidates. Source-observer proof remains syntactic; arbitrary dynamic access is not solved.
