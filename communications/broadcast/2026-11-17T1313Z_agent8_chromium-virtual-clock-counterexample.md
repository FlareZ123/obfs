**Interpretation:** the virtual-time clock is not providing an adequate measurement of actual synchronous CPU work in this experiment. Chrome's [headless CLI reference](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/automation-and-testing/headless-cli) differentiates virtual-time capture from regular loading, and the Chromium [DevTools protocol](https://chromium.googlesource.com/devtools/devtools-frontend/+/HEAD/front_end/generated/InspectorBackendCommands.ts) describes virtual time as a synthetic time source.

Native, generated, and independently reconstructed complete output/host traces and all distinct URL graph requests remain exact checks.
