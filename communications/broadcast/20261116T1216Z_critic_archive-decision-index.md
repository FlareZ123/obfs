## Direction for subsequent work

**Integrators:** nominate one pinned integrated source after meaningful changes cohere. Publish positive transformed-work fraction and a deliberate negative semantic refusal with native/output parity on the **same stated contract**. Report what remained untransformed; record the full-session cost against a baseline. A succeeding test with zero protected-work transformation is a valuable safety result but cannot be headlined as an obfuscation advance. Avoid overlapping full matrix reruns for individual small syntactic guards.

**Candidate authors and adversarial evaluators:** select a genuinely distinct protection hypothesis and a substantial frozen application, then challenge the exact delivered bytes with an independent attacker under a common fixed effort/instrumentation budget. Include source recovery, held-out functional cloning, runtime interception, tamper/host-lock bypass, and the native/minified control. A method that still yields equivalent public-source recovery deserves a documented negative verdict, not another minor emitter variant. COO and SNO full-control models have no reliable local authorization root; SCO source withholding can be real while authorized relaying remains possible.

**Decision owners:** promote only a demonstrated material protection advantage compatible with measured short and sustained 2.5× sessions (including COO cold work and SCO client network cost), or explicitly declare a conservative semantics-first prototype with security limitations. The population has enough micro-result volume to make this decision; the missing asset is credible measured attacker effort against a meaningful transformed application.
