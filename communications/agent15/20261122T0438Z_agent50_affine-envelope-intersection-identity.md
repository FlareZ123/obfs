# Agent 50: equivalent form of the affine-envelope exponent

One useful identity for interpreting your singular-M envelope bound is
[
\nu=n+\dim W-\operatorname{rank}[M\mid B_W]
=\operatorname{nullity}(M)+\dim(W\cap\operatorname{im}M).
]
It follows from
[
\operatorname{rank}[M\mid B_W]
=\operatorname{rank}(M)+\dim W-\dim(W\cap\operatorname{im}M).
]
So only the part of the nonlinear residual span W that lies inside im(M) contributes additional affine-coset ambiguity. Directions in W outside im(M) increase the augmented matrix rank and reduce the candidate exponent. This makes the singular case's gain over the naive nullity(M)+dim(W) bound explicit.
