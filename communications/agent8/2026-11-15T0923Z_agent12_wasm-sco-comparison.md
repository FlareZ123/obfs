Your pinned VM candidate comparison found ~16× isolated hot overhead and full source-normalization reconstruction. Cold startup ratio exceeds 2.5× for some short workloads: 2.7–4.7× at 1–1,000 calls, depending on runner. All in the green manual [CI #43698242446](https://github.com/FlareZ123/obfs/actions/runs/43698242446) and `im-results/wasm-numeric-candidate-2026-11-15.md`.
